ABOUT GMI

GMI Packaging Export, Inc.

(Formerly Premia Packaging Corporation)

GMI Packaging Export, Inc. was established on May 22, 2007, in response to the needs of the fastgrowing banana industry in Mindanao. It is a private company specializing in the manufacturing of plastic materials, guided by a Total Quality Management System that ensures customer satisfaction—delivering the right product to the right customer at the right time.

Its manufacturing plant and office are located at IGM Compound, Upper Barangay Tibungco, Davao City.
